One lesson keeps coming back:"
"More agents are not always better."
"For serious development work, I am seeing much better results from running fewer high-quality Opus sessions than many cheaper or weaker model sessions in parallel."
"The reason is simple."
"Bad output is not cheap if it creates hidden bugs, bad architecture, or hours of cleanup later."
"A weaker model can look productive in the moment."
" But if the result needs constant correction, the real cost moves into review and repair."
"So for now, my conclusion is practical:"
"Use fewer stronger runs where quality matters."
" Use cheaper execution only where the risk is low."
"In agent workflows, throughput is not the same as progress.
