I keep coming back to the same conclusion:"
"For serious development work, the strongest model with the highest effort is often the cheapest option."
"Not per request."
" Not per token."
"But per correct result."
"Every time I reduce quality too much, hidden costs appear quickly:"
" bad assumptions, weaker architecture, missed edge cases, subtle bugs, and cleanup work that costs far more than the original saving."
"A cheaper run can look efficient in the moment."
"Then it creates ten times more work later."
"So my current rule is simple:"
"Use the strongest setup where mistakes are expensive."
" Use cheaper execution only where the risk is low."
"In agentic development, the real cost is not model usage."
"The real cost is bad work that looks finished.
