DYNAMO

Dynamo is my own framework family: a dynamic set of Angular-Material components on the front end, a NodeTS-MongoDB back end, shared full-stack models and a code-generating CLI.

It roughly halves development time on a new project and keeps the functionality stable — a real advantage for a start-up that has no component set of its own. I keep developing it on demand.

Dynamo Builder, built on top of it, lets people who do not develop make simple changes themselves — and makes the whole process easy enough that even complex modifications no longer need a specialist.
